# MyGameImplementation Setup Guide

## Required software

- [Archipelago](https://github.com/ArchipelagoMW/Archipelago/releases)
- The game itself, and a client that talks to Archipelago (fill this in for your game)

## Installing the apworld

1. Download `MyGameImplementation.apworld`.
2. Double-click it, or copy it into the `custom_worlds` folder of your Archipelago install.

## Creating your YAML

Open the Archipelago Launcher and click **Generate Template Options**. Copy
`MyGameImplementation.yaml` from the templates folder, change the options you want and give it to
whoever is generating the multiworld.

## Connecting

Fill in how players start the game and connect it to the Archipelago server.
