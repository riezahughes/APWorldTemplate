# MyGameImplementation

## What does randomization do to this game?

The Crystal Key and the Healing Potion are shuffled into the multiworld. Your own two checks
(the Village Chest and the Dragon's Hoard) can hold items for anyone.

## What is the goal?

Get the Crystal Key, open the Dragon's Hoard and claim the treasure.

## Which items and locations are there?

| Items          | Locations      |
|----------------|----------------|
| Crystal Key    | Village Chest  |
| Healing Potion | Dragon's Hoard |
